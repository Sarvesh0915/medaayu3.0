The problem

Distance makes caregiving harder than it should be.

Elderly parents often live far from their children — medicine adherence and emergencies become silent risks nobody sees coming.

💊

Missed doses

Elderly parents forget medicine timings, especially with multiple daily prescriptions.

📍

Distant families

Children living away have no visibility into whether medicines were actually taken.

⚠️

Emergencies go unnoticed

In a fall or health emergency, there's often no fast way to alert family for help.

The solution

One app, two connected dashboards.

MedAayu links a Child/Caregiver Dashboard with a Parent/Elder Dashboard in real time — so both sides always know what's happening.

Child / Caregiver

Manage from anywhere

  • Add & manage parent's medicines
  • Set schedule, dosage & duration
  • Get alerted if a dose is missed
  • Receive emergency SOS calls & texts
Parent / Elder

Simple by design

  • Loud, simple medicine-time alarms
  • One-tap 'Taken' confirmation
  • Big, easy-to-reach SOS button
  • Auto-calls & messages family in emergency
How it works

From adding a medicine to a confirmed dose — in four steps.

01

Select profile

Choose which parent's medicine plan to manage from your linked family list.

02

Add medicine

Enter medicine name, dosage, and photo for easy recognition.

03

Set schedule

Choose times per day, frequency, and duration of the course.

04

Reminders activate

Alarms are automatically scheduled on the parent's phone.

If a dose is missed

MedAayu keeps you informed — automatically.

No need to call and check. A grace period is given after each alarm, and if it's not confirmed as taken, you're notified instantly — with the medicine name, time, and a one-tap way to call.

Emergency SOS

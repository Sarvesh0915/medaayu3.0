WhatsApp Message Reminders

Gentle nudges for medicines, appointments and check-ups delivered straight to WhatsApp — where your family already is, no new habit required.

The story behind MedAayu

Better healthcare starts with better care

Healthcare is one of the most important parts of our lives, yet managing it often feels unnecessarily complicated. Medical records are scattered across hospitals, prescriptions get misplaced, appointments are forgotten, and families struggle to stay informed about the health of their loved ones. In emergencies, every second matters — but critical information is rarely available when it's needed most.

MedAayu was born from a simple belief: healthcare should be organized, accessible, and there for you when you need it most.

Our vision is to create a single platform where individuals and families can securely manage their entire healthcare journey — from storing medical records and tracking medicines to keeping track of appointments and receiving timely health reminders. We believe technology should reduce stress, not add to it.

As we explored the challenges people face every day, one concern stood out: the safety of elderly family members and those living alone. That's why we're building AI-powered Fall Detection — designed to detect serious falls, notify emergency contacts, and share live location when every second counts. It's one step toward making healthcare more proactive, not just reactive.

At MedAayu, we're not just building another health app. We're building a trusted healthcare companion that puts people first — one that helps families stay connected, promotes healthier lives, and provides peace of mind through secure, intelligent technology.

This is only the beginning. With your support, we're creating a future where managing healthcare is simple, connected, and always within reach.

Welcome to MedAayu — because better healthcare starts with better care.
